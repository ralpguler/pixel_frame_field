3. PIXEL SET (per split x checkpoint; ALL methods scored on the same set)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Omega = GT foreground (face>=0) of the target person
        AND NOT a hidden SMPL-X face (smplx_hidden_faces.npy; training's hidden_mask: these are not supervised)
        AND central-difference stencil valid for the GT correspondence (x+-1 col, x+-1 row all fg, same GT part)
        AND central-difference stencil valid for the PREDICTED correspondence (same predicted part at x+-1 col/row;
            predicted part = argmax of the 24 warped part logits, as eval_512; the mask head is not used)
        AND finite, non-degenerate derived solutions for both the predicted and the GT correspondence
            (sigma_2 of the 2x2 map > 1e-6 * sigma_1).
Report the number of GT-fg pixels dropped at each stage.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. METHODS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
H        rotation head, per-part 6D gathered by the PREDICTED part (geo_e2e readout), bilinear warp to canvas.
H-orc    same, gathered by the GT part (geo_oracle; reference only).
D-<corr>-<disamb>[-<cam>][-r<k>]  frame derived from a correspondence, no rotation head:
  corr   pred = predicted part + uv, uv gathered by the predicted part, mapped through the chart (CSC K=512
                texture = the only (part,uv)->template route in the model; there is no separate XYZ head);
                template normal at the predicted point from a normal texture baked exactly like the CSC texture
                (barycentric blend of area-weighted rest vertex normals).
         gt   = exact GT template point (face + barycentrics, float32) and GT template normal.
  P(x)   per-pixel template point. J(x) = dP/dx (3x2, per canvas px) by least squares over a (2r+1)^2 window,
         using only window pixels of the SAME part (pred part for pred, GT part for gt) that are foreground,
         with a free intercept (plane fit). r in {1,2,3}; all r reported. The main table uses, per derived
         method, the r with the lowest pixel-pooled mean geodesic error on that split for C1 (a test-set choice
         IN THE BASELINE'S FAVOUR).
  A      T = [T1 T2] orthonormal template tangent basis at P (Gram-Schmidt of the template axis least aligned
         with N); A_canvas = (T^T J)^-1 maps tangent coords -> canvas px. Converted to normalised PyTorch3D image
         coords (x left, y up): A_n = diag(-1/f', -1/f') A_canvas, f' = canvas focal.
  cam    persp (default): full perspective, M = [[1,0,-x~],[0,1,-y~]], x~ = -(u+0.5-cx')/f', y~ = -(v+0.5-cy')/f'
         from the canvas intrinsics (source cam_int cropped to gt_box: f' = f*512/side, c' = (c-g0)*512/side;
         BEDLAM2 cam_int from correspondence/posed3d/<job>.npz by uid; MAMMA cam_int from the source .data.pyd).
         weak: M = [I 0]. Reported as a secondary row.
  solve  A_n = s M [c1 c2], c1 = R T1, c2 = R T2 orthonormal, s unknown. Closed form: with d the unit ray, E an
         orthonormal basis of d-perp and G = M E, A' = G^-1 A_n = U diag(s1,s2) V^T; then
         [c1 c2] = E (A'/s1) + d t^T with t = +-sqrt(1-(s2/s1)^2) v2. TWO solutions (the sign of t: tilt/Necker
         ambiguity). R_derived = [c1 c2 c1xc2][T1 T2 N]^T. Solver verified on synthetic rotations first
         (weak and perspective): one of the two solutions must recover R to < 1e-3 deg.
  disamb orc    = per pixel the solution closer (geodesic) to GT. UPPER BOUND, in the baseline's favour.
         part   = GT-free: per image and per part (pred part for pred, GT part for gt), RANSAC over the 2
                  candidates of up to 64 random pixels (seed 0): score = #pixels with a candidate within 20 deg;
                  the best hypothesis is refined 3x by the chordal mean of the inliers' closer candidates;
                  every pixel takes the candidate closer to that part reference (rigid-part prior: the true
                  solution is ~constant over a rigid part, the mirror solution varies with the template normal).
         smooth = GT-free: 'part' labels, then 6 checkerboard ICM sweeps: each pixel takes the candidate with the
                  smaller summed chordal distance to its 8 same-part neighbours' current choices.
         Pre-declared primary practical rule: smooth.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. METRICS (degrees)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
geodesic  angle of R_gt^T R_pred.
normal    angle between R_pred N and R_gt N, N = GT template normal at the GT surface point (same N for every
          method, so it isolates R). = swing angle of the swing-twist split of R_gt^T R_pred about N.
twist     twist angle of R_gt^T R_pred about N: quaternion (w,v), w>=0: 2*atan2(|v.N|, w). = the rotation about the
          GT normal left after aligning the normals by the minimal rotation.
Statistics: mean / median / p90, pixel-pooled over Omega AND over per-image means (mean of per-image means,
plus median/p90 of per-image means).
Ambiguity: share of Omega where the two derived solutions differ by > 30 deg (pred and gt correspondence).
Paired per image (per-image mean geodesic over Omega): H vs best practical D-pred, H vs D-pred-orc:
wins/losses, two-sided sign test, mean difference with a 95% bootstrap CI (10,000 image resamples, seed 0).
Breakdowns: GT part group (torso 1-2, hands+feet 3-6, legs 7-14, arms 15-22, head 23-24); distance to the
target person's GT silhouette in canvas px (Euclidean distance transform of face>=0): <4, 4-12, >=12;
garment vs body: NO garment label exists in these shards (GT is the SMPL-X body) -> not reported.
