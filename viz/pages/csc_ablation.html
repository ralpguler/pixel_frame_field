SETUP

What was run

The CSC operator maps predicted (part, u, v) differentiably to canonical template XYZ (one 3D grid-sample over a baked 512² per-part texture; round-trip median 0.000 mm), and the loss supervises that map and its image-space gradient against GT — MiDaS-style gradient matching applied to correspondences. Ambiguous head texels (ear/jaw fold, 12%) are masked, not supervised.

THE SEAM CLAIM

Gradient matching did its job

Across a true GT part boundary the body is physically continuous, so a predicted XYZ step >30 mm there is a chart-jump artifact — exactly what the image-space gradient term targets.

Confirmed

The seam-break rate halves and the median seam step (3.51 mm vs GT 3.69 mm) is essentially exact. The interior gradient residual — the MiDaS-style term's direct target — drops 31%. The loss delivered precisely its design goal.

E's epitaph

Soft gather is measurably worse at seams (1.99% vs 1.47%) — mixing charts across a boundary blurs the very transition it was hypothesised to help. D shows GT parts are not needed at train time; C stays the working checkpoint.

WHAT DIDN'T MOVE

Rotations and discontinuity placement are part-bounded

The rotation jump structure is identical across all four runs: predictions are smoother than GT in the interior (0.59° vs 0.89° per edge), step only ~1.0° across missed true seams (GT: 29°), and step ~31° at spurious ones (GT: 1.6°). Spurious ≈ missed (1.28% vs 1.36%) — the model places roughly the right number of discontinuities at slightly wrong positions. With part accuracy frozen at 95.4% everywhere, every remaining large-error mode — the misplaced discontinuities, the p99 surface tail, the residual +1.3 mm boundary band — traces to the part head, not to UV or rotation quality.

Next lever 1 · part boundary

--rot-dilate (supervise each part's rotation in a dilated band beyond its mask) is implemented and idle; part-boundary localisation losses are the other candidate.

Next lever 2 · hidden geometry

The eye/lip speckle is trained-in: 1–3% of head pixels carry SMPL-X eyeball/mouth-interior labels (verified on GT). Fix is loader-level (mask pixels whose GT face index is hidden geometry out of part/UV/rot losses) + a fine-tune from C — no shard regeneration.

REAL-DOMAIN CHECK

COCO minival — the CSC loss is also a sim2real regulariser

Official DensePose GPS eval (GT boxes, minival; job 5668430). COCO annotations are v1-chart coordinates, so face_uv_v2 checkpoints get an exact v2→v1 remap on hands/feet (baked through face+barycentrics, round-trip 0.001 chart units) before scoring.

The unexpected finding

25k extra steps of pure synthetic fine-tuning (A) degraded real-domain COCO by 1.3 AP — the known sim2real drift. The CSC loss recovered that drift and landed at-or-slightly-above baseline (+1.6 AP over A). On real data it behaves as a regulariser against synthetic overfitting, not merely a synthetic-metric win.

Method note

The remap is not cosmetic: scoring v2-chart predictions raw against v1-chart annotations costs 4.7 AP — enough to have flipped the conclusion. Chart conventions must travel with checkpoints.

CONTEXT

Where the absolute level comes from — the recipe lineage, decomposed

The ~0.57 range is well below the earlier IUV-only model (dp_v2_L512, 0.633) and the COCO-fine-tuned champion (0.73–0.74). Riza challenged the gap, so every step of the orient recipe was re-scored with the authoritative GT-box GPS at matched step 58k (job 5675674) — separating what the COCO sparse-point proxy had blurred together at decision time.

The rotation task is free — the recipe wasn't

Adding orientation cost 0.3 AP (hflip loss included). Synthetic rotation geo improved monotonically down the ladder while real AP fell — they anti-correlate on the backbone-LR axis. LLRD at 3e-4 is the only step that improved both; the real cost sits in the per-part head (−1.8) and the 1e-3 base LR (−3.4), which trains DINOv3's top blocks 25× hotter and overwrites its real-image features with the synthetic manifold.

Why the decision looked safe

The LR choice did weigh correspondence and COCO: at 58k the sparse proxy read flat 0.784 / 3e-4 0.788 / 1e-3 0.782 pt_acc — correct ranking (matches GPS exactly) but 6× magnitude compression, so a 3.4 AP loss read as noise. Sparse point accuracy is nearly blind to what GPS measures. Real-domain sweet spot in this family: per-part + LLRD 3e-4; recovery lever for deployment: joint BEDLAM+COCO fine-tune (worth +11 AP on dp_v3).
